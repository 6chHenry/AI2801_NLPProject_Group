\begin{abstract}
KV caching limits long-context inference on memory and latency.
We present \textbf{TieredKV}, a training-free stack combining attention-sink protection, optional cross-layer importance fusion, and TreeKV intra-layer eviction under a \textbf{Unified Hierarchical Budget (UHB)}.
On Pythia-70M with a \emph{matched} compress-and-evaluate PPL protocol, \texttt{sink\_treekv} at $\rho{=}0.5$ halves KV length (4096$\rightarrow$2066) and raises throughput ${\sim}31\%$ while increasing WikiText-2 PPL by ${\sim}6\%$ and PG-19 (20 books) by only ${\sim}1.4\%$.
At $\rho{=}0.8$, both corpora stay within ${\sim}1.5\%$ of full KV with smaller memory/speed wins.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Transformer inference stores growing KV caches.
Prior work targets attention sinks~\cite{xiao2024streamingllm}, inter-layer sharing~\cite{sun2024yoco}, and intra-layer eviction (SnapKV~\cite{li2024snapkv}, TreeKV~\cite{he2025treekv}).
We compose three \textbf{training-free} tiers on Pythia-70M~\cite{biderman2023pythia}:
\textbf{(T1)} sink-token protection;
\textbf{(T2)} optional cross-layer attention fusion before selection;
\textbf{(T3)} TreeKV block top-$k$.
UHB keeps \emph{equal compressed length across layers} for correct decoding.

\textbf{Contributions.}
(1)~UHB formalizing sink, block, and local-window budgets;
(2)~ablation pipeline with matched fp32/eager evaluation;
(3)~20-book PG-19 study under a unified compress-and-evaluate protocol;
(4)~joint quality \emph{and} systems tables including full-KV baseline latency/KV length.

\section{Method}

After prefill on $x_{1:S}$, each layer's cache is compressed once using prefill attentions; RoPE uses kept indices only.
Budget $B=\lfloor\rho(S-k_{\mathrm{sink}}-w_{\mathrm{local}})\rfloor$; local window always kept.
\textbf{T1:} retain $\mathcal{S}=\{0,\ldots,k_{\mathrm{sink}}-1\}$.
\textbf{T2:} fuse layer-wise importances $I_\ell$ (from last $w_{\mathrm{obs}}$ queries) via shallow-weighted average, shared across layers; DALB geometry is ablated separately.
\textbf{T3:} split history into $N$ blocks; budget $b_i\propto 2^{i\gamma}$; top-$b_i$ tokens per block; capacity-reassign so $\sum b_i=B$ and all layers share equal length.

\section{Experiments}

\subsection{Protocol}
\textbf{Model:} Pythia-70M; \textbf{all methods use fp32 + eager}.
\textbf{PPL (matched for all rows):} for each 512-token target chunk, prefill context (up to 1536 tokens), optionally compress KV once, then forward the target with explicit \texttt{position\_ids} ($L{=}2048$, stride 512).
Full KV uses the same loop with a no-op compressor.
\textbf{PG-19:} 20 test books, 8192 tokens each; token-weighted mean $\pm$ std over books.
\textbf{Latency:} 4096-token PG-19 prefill + 100-token greedy decode.
\textbf{Defaults:} $k_{\mathrm{sink}}{=}4$, $w_{\mathrm{obs}}{=}w_{\mathrm{local}}{=}32$, $N{=}4$; sweep $\rho\in\{0.5,0.8\}$.

\subsection{Perplexity}
Table~\ref{tab:ppl} reports matched-protocol PPL.
At $\rho{=}0.5$, \texttt{sink\_treekv} adds ${\sim}6\%$ WikiText overhead but only ${\sim}1.4\%$ on PG-19 (36.67$\rightarrow$37.17).
At $\rho{=}0.8$, both corpora stay within ${\sim}1.5\%$ of full KV.
Earlier ${\sim}2.5\times$ PG-19 gaps came from mixing a 2048-token sliding-window baseline with a compress-and-evaluate protocol for compressed runs.
Inter-layer fusion does not beat \texttt{sink\_treekv}.

\subsection{Compression-ratio trade-off}
Table~\ref{tab:pareto} summarizes \texttt{sink\_treekv} at $\rho{=}0.5$ vs.\ $\rho{=}0.8$.
Raising $\rho$ nearly closes the WikiText gap (+1.5\%) but yields modest PG-19 gains (+0.4\% at $\rho{=}0.8$) because TreeKV's geometric ``sparse-left'' policy still prunes distant blocks aggressively.
Systems-wise, $\rho{=}0.8$ retains 3284 tokens vs.\ 2066 at $\rho{=}0.5$; throughput gain falls from +31\% to +3\% (within-run baselines).

\subsection{Systems metrics}
Table~\ref{tab:sys} reports full-KV baseline and compressors on identical 4096-token prefills ($\rho{=}0.5$).
KV length halves; TTFT drops 31\%; end-to-end throughput +31\%; decode FLOPs fall ${\sim}49\%$.
At $\rho{=}0.8$, KV length is 3284 (+3\% throughput; Table~\ref{tab:pareto}).
Peak memory is higher for compressors because prefill uses \texttt{output\_attentions=True}; KV \emph{length}---not peak GB---is the fair storage metric here.

\subsection{Discussion}
\textbf{Best config:} \texttt{sink\_treekv}---matches TreeKV PPL with stable TTFT benefit from sinks.
\textbf{Choosing $\rho$:} $\rho{=}0.5$ maximizes memory/latency savings; $\rho{=}0.8$ is near-lossless under our protocol.
\textbf{Evaluation lesson:} PPL numbers are protocol-dependent; always match baseline and compressor pipelines.
\textbf{PG-19 variance:} high per-book std ($\pm$12) remains; report multi-book averages.

\section{Conclusion}
TieredKV integrates three training-free compression tiers under UHB.
On Pythia-70M with matched evaluation, $\rho{=}0.5$ delivers ${\sim}2\times$ shorter caches and ${\sim}31\%$ higher throughput at ${\sim}6\%$ WikiText / ${\sim}1.4\%$ PG-19 cost; $\rho{=}0.8$ is near-lossless with smaller systems wins.
Reproduce via \texttt{python src/run\_pg19\_suite.py --k\_ratio \{0.5,0.8\} --num\_samples 20}; results in \texttt{results/results\_pg19\_suite\_r\{050,080\}.json}.
